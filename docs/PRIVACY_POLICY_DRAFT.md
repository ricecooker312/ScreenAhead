# CareCompass Privacy Notice: DRAFT

> This is a plain-language starting point written for the app. It **must be reviewed by a lawyer** and adapted to the operator, the jurisdictions and the actual vendors before use.

**Last updated:** [date] · **Who we are:** [operator legal name, address, contact email]

## The short version
- We keep your health answers so we can guide you.
- We never sell your information.
- We never use it for ads.
- Doctors only see what you choose to share.
- You can download or delete your information at any time.

## What we collect
- **Account details:** email, first name (optional), password (stored scrambled using a one-way hash).
- **Health information you choose to enter:** questionnaire answers, symptoms, medicines, allergies, health and family history, appointments.
- **Messages** between you and clinicians you connect with.
- **Technical data:** basic server logs (time, address of the request, the result). Logs do not contain your health answers.

## Why we use it
- To show you next steps, education and specialists.
- To let you share a summary or connect with your clinician, **only if you choose to**.
- To keep the service secure (for example, to stop break-in attempts).

## Who we share it with
- **Clinicians you connect with**, limited to what you pick. You can stop sharing at any time.
- **Service providers** that host the app for us (for example, cloud hosting and email), under contracts that protect your data. [List vendors.]
- **When the law requires it.**

We do **not** share identifiable health data with advertisers or data brokers.

## How long we keep it
Until you delete your account. Security logs are kept for [period]. Backups are erased within [period].

## Your choices and rights
- Download your data: Settings → Download my data.
- Delete your account: Settings → Delete my account.
- Change what a clinician can see, or disconnect: Me → Care team.
- Depending on where you live, you may have more rights. Contact [email].

CareCompass is for adults 18 and over. [Adjust as needed.]
